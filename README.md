## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/People%20with%20professions/Man%20Technologist%20Light%20Skin%20Tone.png" width="28" /> &nbsp;About Me

> *"I code until it clicks. Then break it, just to learn more."*

I'm **Korrapolu Eswar Adithya**, a B.Tech student passionate about **VLSI Design & Verification**. I specialize in building digital hardware from the ground up — from **RTL design** in Verilog/SystemVerilog to **functional verification** using **UVM** methodology and **AMBA protocol** IP verification.

- 🔬 &nbsp;Currently deep-diving into **UVM-based IP Verification** and **AMBA protocol design**
- 🏗️ &nbsp;Built **100+ RTL design projects** including FIR filters, sequence detectors, and alarm clocks
- 🧪 &nbsp;Experienced in **SystemVerilog constrained-random verification** & **coverage-driven methodologies**
- 🔩 &nbsp;Designed **CMOS circuits** — from ADCs using VCOs to sequence detectors in 90nm technology
- ⚡ &nbsp;Also proficient in **Java full-stack development**, **Embedded/IoT**, and **FPGA design (VHDL)**
- 🎯 &nbsp;Goal: Contribute to next-generation **SoC design & verification** in the semiconductor industry
